24 Hours In

24 Hours In

Stone Town

Zanzibar, Tanzania

Breakfast

7:30 AM
Lukmaan Restaurant

Vitumbua (rice pancakes) with chai in the narrow streets. Simple, and exactly what you want before a morning of walking.

Things to Do

9:00 AM
The Old Fort

Start at the 17th-century fort. Free to enter, the open-air amphitheatre inside is worth seeing.

Wander at Stone Town

Get lost on purpose. Carved Zanzibari doors, spice shops, alleys that open into courtyards. Don't use a map.

Places to See

11:00 AM
Changuu Island/Prison Island

Changuu Island, a serene escape steeped in history and natural beauty. Known as Prison Island, this captivating destination offers more than just a glimpse into Zanzibar’s past

Christ Church Cathedral/Slave Market

Consecrated in 1879, Christ Church Cathedral in Stone Town, Zanzibar, stands as a powerful testament to the end of East Africa’s largest slave market, upon whose grounds it was built.

Lunch

1:30 PM
Zanzibar Coffee House

Climb to the rooftop for panoramic views over tin rooftops and minarets. The seafood curry is excellent.

More Activities

3:00 PM
Spice Farm Tour

Take a guided tour of a traditional Zanzibar spice farm to learn about the aromatic plants and their uses in local cuisine.

Sunset at the Waterfront

Walk back to Forodhani Gardens. Sunset over the Indian Ocean with silhouettes of dhows. Find a spot on the seawall.

Dinner

7:00 PM
Forodhani Night Market

Fresh seafood, Zanzibar pizza, sugar cane juice, skewered mishkaki. Walk the full row first, then commit.

CapeTown Fish Market's Bar

End the night on this rooftop overlooking the harbour. Cold Kilimanjaro lager, live taarab music, sea breeze.

24 Hours In

Nanyuki

Nanyuki, Kenya

Breakfast

7:00 AM
Barney's Bar and Restaurant

A cozy spot just next to the Nanyuki airstrip for breakfast and lunch, known for its friendly atmosphere and delicious cuisine.

Things to Do

9:00 AM
Drive to Root North Farmshop

A scenic drive through canola plantations in Timau to this quaint farmshop, where you can sample fresh produce and local delicacies.

Ngare Ndare Forest

A lush forest area away from the city perfect for canopy walks, bird watching and a swim at the perfectly blue waterfalls.

Ol Pejeta Conservancy

11:00 AM
Ol Pejeta Conservancy

A 20-minute drive from Nanyuki, the conservancy is home to the last two remaining northern white rhinos. Also, it offers excellent opportunities for wildlife viewing and photography.

Lunch

1:00 PM
Morani's Restaurant

Named after Ol Pejeta's beloved rhino ambassador, Morani's is a popular stop at the Conservancy's Information Centre, known for its hearty burgers made with locally sourced Ol Pejeta beef.

Evening Game Drive

3:00 PM
Evening Game Drive at Ol Pejeta Conservancy

Experience the thrill of spotting wildlife as the sun sets during an evening game drive through the conservancy.

Dinner

7:30 PM
Jib's Café Bistro

Nestled in the heart of Nanyuki Town along Kenyatta Highway, Jib’s Café Bistro is a vibrant, two-story hotspot that perfectly balances a cozy rustic charm with a modern lifestyle vibe

24 Hours In

Suswa

Kenya

Breakfast

8:00 AM
Quick Bite at Artcaffe Sigona

Grab a quick bite at Artcaffe Sigona just inside Rubis PS Sigona. They serve okay coffee and light pastries.

Things to Do

9:30 AM
Bike Ride or Game Drive at Hells Gate National Park

Enjoy a scenic bike ride or a thrilling game drive through the stunning landscapes of Hells Gate National Park.

Hike at Mt Longonot

If you are up for a challenge, take a hike up Mt Longonot for breathtaking views of the surrounding landscape.

Places to See

12:00 PM
Visit the Cafe Ubuntu community

The community hub offers a space for the locals to learn sewing and beading. They also have a small shop selling handmade crafts.

Lunch

1:30 PM
Cafe Ubuntu

A cozy community space where you can enjoy great coffee and light meals while supporting local artisans.

More Activities

5:30 PM
Catch the sunset at Ewaso Kedong

Enjoy the stunning views of the sun setting over the hills and landscape.

Dinner

7:30 PM
Nyama Choma zone

A couple of places offering authentic Kenyan grilled meat dishes in a casual setting.

24 Hours In

Diani

Kwale, Kenya

Breakfast

7:30 AM
Sunnyside Up

Feet in the sand, fresh fruit platter, eggs any way you want them. The Indian Ocean is right there. Best way to start a Diani morning.

Things to Do

9:00 AM
Dolphin Watching

The best place to see dolphins in their natural habitat is along the coast in Kisite Mpunguti Marine Park in the early morning.

Kite Surfing

Experience the thrill of kite surfing along the pristine beaches of Diani. Harness the power of the wind and glide across the turquoise waters.

Places to See

11:30 AM
Visit a gallery

Explore the local art scene at one of Diani's many galleries. Discover works by emerging artists and learn about the region's rich cultural heritage.

Lunch

1:00 PM
The Salty Squid Beach Bar & Restaurant

A restaurant at beach front with stunning views of the ocean. It offers a great selection of fantastic food & cocktails. Make a reservation in advance.

More Activities

3:30 PM
Boat ride along the coast

Diani has some of the best boat ride experiences in the region. Enjoy scenic views and spot marine life like starfish.

Sunset Beach Walk at Kongo River

Walk south along the beach at low tide. White sand, warm water, and nobody around. The light at 5pm is incredible for photography.

Dinner

7:00 PM
Nomad Beach Bar

Right on the sand, lanterns and fairy lights, grilled calamari and cocktails. The kind of place where dinner turns into the whole night.
