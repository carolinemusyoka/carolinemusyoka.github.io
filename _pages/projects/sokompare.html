Sokompare

The Problem

Online shopping across multiple retailers means prices for the same product can vary wildly. Manually checking each site is tedious, and by the time you decide, the price may have changed. Consumers need a way to see all prices at once, understand pricing trends, and know when to buy — not just what's cheapest right now, but what will be cheapest soon.

How It Works

01

Price Aggregation

Sokompare scrapes and aggregates product prices from multiple online retailers in real-time. Search for any product and instantly see a side-by-side comparison of prices, shipping costs, and availability across all supported stores.

02

ML Price Prediction

A scikit-learn powered model analyzes historical price data to predict future trends. The system identifies seasonal patterns, sale cycles, and pricing anomalies — telling you whether to buy now or wait for a likely price drop.

03

Price History & Tracking

Every product's price is tracked over time with visual charts showing highs, lows, and averages. Set a target price and get notified the moment any retailer drops below your threshold — never miss a deal again.

04

Smart Recommendations

Based on your search history and price alerts, Sokompare surfaces deals you're likely to care about. The recommendation engine learns your preferences without requiring an account or collecting personal data.
